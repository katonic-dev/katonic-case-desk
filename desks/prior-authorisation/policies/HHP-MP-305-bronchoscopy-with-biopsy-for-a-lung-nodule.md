# Harbourline Health Plan (an invented health plan, for demonstration only)
# Coverage policy HHP-MP-305: Bronchoscopy with biopsy for a lung nodule

Version 1.3. Owner: Medical Director. Approved by the Medical Policy Committee, 20 April 2026. Next review: April 2027.

This policy was written for a software demonstration. It is not clinical guidance and no real health plan uses it.

## 1. What this policy covers

1.1 This policy sets out when the plan authorises bronchoscopy with transbronchial biopsy for an adult member with a lung nodule.

1.2 It covers a diagnosis of a solitary pulmonary nodule or a lung mass. Other reasons for bronchoscopy are outside this policy.

## 2. Criteria for authorisation

All of the criteria in 2.1 to 2.5 must be met.

2.1 A CT scan in the last 60 days shows a lung nodule of 8 mm or more.

2.2 The nodule has at least one of these: growth on serial imaging, uptake on a PET scan, or a shape that raises suspicion of cancer.

2.3 The member's lung function has been tested in the last 90 days, or the request states that the member is fit for the procedure.

2.4 The requesting clinician is a respiratory physician or a thoracic surgeon, or the request records a referral to one.

2.5 The request records that the risks of the procedure were discussed and that the member consents.

## 3. What the reviewer does

3.1 A clinical reviewer may authorise a request that meets every criterion.

3.2 Where the request does not record enough to judge a criterion, the reviewer asks the provider for the missing information.

3.3 A request that does not meet a criterion is referred to the Medical Director. Only the Medical Director decides against a request.

## 4. Urgency

4.1 A request under this policy is treated as expedited where the request records a suspicion of cancer.

## 5. What this policy does not cover

5.1 Surgical lung biopsy.

5.2 Biopsy guided by CT through the chest wall, which is covered by a separate policy.

5.3 Members under 18.

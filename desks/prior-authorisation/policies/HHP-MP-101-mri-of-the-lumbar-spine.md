# Harbourline Health Plan (an invented health plan, for demonstration only)
# Coverage policy HHP-MP-101: MRI of the lumbar spine

Version 3.0. Owner: Medical Director. Approved by the Medical Policy Committee, 12 January 2026. Next review: January 2027.

This policy was written for a software demonstration. It is not clinical guidance and no real health plan uses it.

## 1. What this policy covers

1.1 This policy sets out when the plan authorises magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) of the lumbar spine for an adult member with low back pain, with or without leg pain.

1.2 It applies to requests made outside an emergency department or an inpatient stay.

## 2. Criteria for authorisation

All of the criteria in 2.1 to 2.4 must be met, unless section 3 applies.

2.1 The member has had low back pain, with or without leg pain, for at least six weeks.

2.2 The member has completed at least six weeks of conservative treatment in the last six months. Conservative treatment means a course of supervised physiotherapy or a documented home exercise programme, together with pain relief medicine unless it is unsuitable for the member.

2.3 The request records a physical examination in the last 30 days, including a neurological examination of the legs.

2.4 The request says how the result of the MRI is expected to change the member's care, for example a referral for surgery or an injection.

## 3. When the six weeks do not apply

3.1 The criteria in 2.1 and 2.2 do not apply where the request records any of these: new loss of bladder or bowel control, numbness in the saddle area, progressive weakness in a leg, suspected infection, suspected cancer, or significant injury.

3.2 A request under 3.1 is treated as expedited.

## 4. What the reviewer does

4.1 A clinical reviewer may authorise a request that meets every criterion.

4.2 Where the request does not record enough to judge a criterion, the reviewer asks the provider for the missing information.

4.3 A request that does not meet a criterion is referred to the Medical Director. Only the Medical Director decides against a request.

## 5. What this policy does not cover

5.1 MRI of the cervical or thoracic spine.

5.2 Repeat MRI within 12 months of an earlier lumbar MRI.

5.3 Members under 18.

# Harbourline Health Plan (an invented health plan, for demonstration only)
# Coverage policy HHP-MP-214: Continuous glucose monitors

Version 2.2. Owner: Medical Director. Approved by the Medical Policy Committee, 9 February 2026. Next review: February 2027.

This policy was written for a software demonstration. It is not clinical guidance and no real health plan uses it.

## 1. What this policy covers

1.1 This policy sets out when the plan authorises a continuous glucose monitor, its sensors and its reader for a member with diabetes.

1.2 It applies to a first request. Renewals are covered in section 4.

## 2. Criteria for authorisation

All of the criteria in 2.1 to 2.5 must be met.

2.1 The member has a diagnosis of diabetes.

2.2 The member is treated with insulin, by three or more injections a day or by an insulin pump.

2.3 The member tests capillary glucose four or more times a day, shown by a meter download or a log covering at least the last 30 days.

2.4 The member has completed a structured diabetes education course.

2.5 The prescriber has seen the member in person or by video in the last six months to assess their diabetes care.

## 3. What the reviewer does

3.1 A clinical reviewer may authorise a request that meets every criterion.

3.2 Where the request does not record enough to judge a criterion, the reviewer asks the provider for the missing information.

3.3 A request that does not meet a criterion is referred to the Medical Director. Only the Medical Director decides against a request.

## 4. Renewal

4.1 Authorisation lasts 12 months.

4.2 A renewal needs a record that the member has used the monitor and has been seen by the prescriber in the last six months.

## 5. What this policy does not cover

5.1 Members who do not use insulin.

5.2 A second monitor while the first is in its authorised period.

5.3 Insulin pumps, which are covered by a separate policy.

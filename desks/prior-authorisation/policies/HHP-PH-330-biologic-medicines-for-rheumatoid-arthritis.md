# Harbourline Health Plan (an invented health plan, for demonstration only)
# Coverage policy HHP-PH-330: Biologic medicines for rheumatoid arthritis

Version 4.1. Owner: Chief Pharmacist. Approved by the Pharmacy and Therapeutics Committee, 16 March 2026. Next review: March 2027.

This policy was written for a software demonstration. It is not clinical guidance and no real health plan uses it.

## 1. What this policy covers

1.1 This policy sets out when the plan authorises a first biologic medicine for an adult member with rheumatoid arthritis.

1.2 The plan's preferred first biologic is an adalimumab biosimilar.

## 2. Criteria for authorisation

All of the criteria in 2.1 to 2.5 must be met.

2.1 A rheumatologist has made the diagnosis of rheumatoid arthritis and is the prescriber.

2.2 The member has active disease, shown by a disease activity score (DAS28) above 5.1 recorded in the last 30 days.

2.3 The member has tried methotrexate at a dose of at least 15 mg a week for at least three months, unless methotrexate is unsuitable for the member and the request says why.

2.4 The member has tried a second conventional medicine (sulfasalazine, leflunomide or hydroxychloroquine), alone or with methotrexate, for at least three months, unless it is unsuitable for the member and the request says why.

2.5 The request records the results of screening for tuberculosis and hepatitis B in the last 12 months.

## 3. What the reviewer does

3.1 A clinical reviewer may authorise a request that meets every criterion.

3.2 Where the request does not record enough to judge a criterion, the reviewer asks the provider for the missing information.

3.3 A request that does not meet a criterion is referred to the Medical Director. Only the Medical Director decides against a request.

## 4. Length of authorisation

4.1 A first authorisation lasts six months.

4.2 Renewal needs a record of the disease activity score after at least 12 weeks of treatment.

## 5. What this policy does not cover

5.1 A switch from one biologic medicine to another.

5.2 Biologic medicines for conditions other than rheumatoid arthritis.

5.3 Members under 18.

# Meridian Trust Bank (an invented bank, for demonstration only)
# Policy MTB-POL-021: Customer information and data sharing

Version 3.0. Owner: Chief Data Officer. Approved by the Board, 12 January 2026. Next review: January 2027.

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This policy sets out how the bank collects, holds, shares and disposes of customer information. It applies to every employee, contractor and service provider who handles it.

## 2. Collection and purpose

2.1 Customer information is collected for a stated purpose and used only for that purpose or one the customer has agreed to.

2.2 The bank records the purpose for each category of information it holds.

## 3. Consent

3.1 The bank obtains a customer's consent before sharing their information with a third party, except where the law requires the sharing.

3.2 Consent is recorded with the date, the channel and what it covers. A customer can withdraw consent through any channel the bank offers, and the withdrawal takes effect within two working days.

## 4. Sharing with third parties

4.1 Information is shared with a third party only under a written agreement that covers purpose, security, retention and onward sharing.

4.2 Information leaves the bank's systems only through a channel approved by Information Security.

4.3 The bank keeps a record of each sharing arrangement: the recipient, the categories of information, the purpose and the legal basis.

## 5. Customer access and correction

5.1 A customer may ask for a copy of the information the bank holds about them, and the bank answers within 30 days.

5.2 A customer may ask for a correction. Where the bank disagrees, it records the request beside the information.

## 6. Security and disposal

6.1 Customer information is encrypted at rest and in transit.

6.2 Information is kept for the period set in the retention schedule and then disposed of securely.

## 7. What this policy does not cover

7.1 The bank does not currently share customer information with other financial institutions at the customer's request through a shared platform. Such sharing would need a consent mechanism, a data format and a dispute process that this policy does not define.

# Policy library of an invented bank

Meridian Trust Bank does not exist. These three policies were written for the
demo of the regulatory change desk so that a gap analysis has something to
compare against. They are labelled as invented on every page.

On the platform: in Knowledge, choose Connect Source, then File Upload, add the
three files under the source name "Meridian policy library (invented)", and
attach that source in the Regulatory Monitoring Agent's Knowledge section.
Indexing needs an embedding model registered in AI Gateway.

Each policy ends with a "What this policy does not cover" section. Those gaps
are deliberate: they are what the two demo publications (MAS, 7 October 2026;
Bank Negara Malaysia, 17 November 2026) will be found to bear on.

# Meridian Trust Bank (an invented bank, for demonstration only)
# Policy MTB-POL-014: Artificial intelligence and model risk

Version 2.1. Owner: Head of Model Risk. Approved by the Risk Committee, 3 March 2026. Next review: March 2027.

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This policy sets out how the bank governs models, including models that use machine learning and generative artificial intelligence. It applies to every model used in a decision that affects a customer, a financial position or a regulatory report.

1.2 A "model" is a quantitative method that turns inputs into estimates or decisions. A generative AI system used to draft text for a person to review is a model for the purposes of this policy where its output reaches a customer or a regulator.

## 2. Governance

2.1 The Risk Committee owns model risk. The Head of Model Risk maintains the model inventory and reports to the Committee each quarter.

2.2 Every model has a named owner, a named validator and an entry in the inventory before first use.

2.3 Accountability for a model's outcomes rests with the business owner, not the developer and not the vendor.

## 3. Inventory and materiality

3.1 The inventory records, for each model, its purpose, owner, validator, materiality tier, data sources, last validation date and known limitations.

3.2 Materiality has three tiers. Tier 1 models affect customer outcomes directly or feed regulatory reporting. Tier 2 models inform a decision a person takes. Tier 3 models are exploratory.

3.3 Models obtained from a vendor are in the inventory on the same terms as models built in the bank.

## 4. Development and validation

4.1 A Tier 1 or Tier 2 model is validated by someone independent of its development before first use and after any material change.

4.2 Validation covers data quality, conceptual soundness, performance against a benchmark, and fairness where the model affects individuals.

4.3 Generative AI models used in Tier 1 or Tier 2 work are tested for unsupported statements against a sample of their outputs before first use. The sample size and the acceptable rate are set by the validator.

## 5. Use and monitoring

5.1 A Tier 1 model's outputs are monitored monthly against the thresholds set at validation. A breach is reported to the model owner within five working days.

5.2 A person reviews every customer-facing output of a generative AI model before it is sent. The reviewer's name is recorded.

5.3 Staff are told which systems use AI and what the system may and may not do.

## 6. Third parties

6.1 Where a model or an AI service is obtained from a vendor, the contract gives the bank the right to information needed to validate and monitor it.

## 7. What this policy does not cover

7.1 Systems that act without a person's review ("autonomous agents") are not permitted under this policy. A proposal to use one is a material change under 4.1 and needs the Committee's approval.

7.2 This policy does not set out how customers are told that AI was used in a decision about them.

7.3 This policy does not require the board to receive a report on AI risk; the Risk Committee receives it.

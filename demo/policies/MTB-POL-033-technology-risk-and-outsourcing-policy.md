# Meridian Trust Bank (an invented bank, for demonstration only)
# Policy MTB-POL-033: Technology risk and outsourcing

Version 1.4. Owner: Chief Information Officer. Approved by the Risk Committee, 20 May 2026. Next review: May 2027.

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This policy sets out how the bank manages the risks of its technology and of services it obtains from others.

## 2. Critical systems

2.1 A system is critical where its failure for more than four hours would stop a customer service or a regulatory obligation.

2.2 Every critical system has a named owner, a recovery time objective and a tested recovery plan. Recovery is tested at least once a year.

## 3. Changes to technology

3.1 A change to a critical system goes through change control, with a rollback plan and a named approver.

3.2 Before first use of a public cloud service or an emerging technology in a critical system, the bank completes a risk assessment and informs its regulator where the regulator requires it.

## 4. Incidents

4.1 A technology incident that affects customers is reported to the Head of Operational Risk within one hour of being detected, and to the regulator within the period the regulator sets.

## 5. Outsourcing

5.1 An outsourcing arrangement for a critical service is approved by the Risk Committee and covered by a written agreement that gives the bank audit rights, exit rights and the right to be told of sub-contracting.

5.2 The bank keeps a register of outsourcing arrangements, reviewed yearly.

## 6. Access and security

6.1 Access to systems follows least privilege and is reviewed quarterly.

6.2 Multi-factor authentication is required for every staff account that reaches a critical system.

## 7. What this policy does not cover

7.1 This policy does not set out how the bank assesses a provider's own use of artificial intelligence in a service it supplies to the bank.
